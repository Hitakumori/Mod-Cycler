# Third-party components

The Windows executable includes Python 3.12.10, Tcl/Tk 8.6.15, Pillow 10.4.0, pystray 0.19.5, six 1.17.0, and the PyInstaller 6.22.3 bootloader/runtime hooks.

Their license texts are included in `licenses/` in the source repository and portable ZIP. The PyInstaller text includes its bootloader exception. These notices describe third-party components and do not assign a license to Mod Cycler's own source.

Upstream source:

- Python: https://github.com/python/cpython/tree/v3.12.10
- Tcl: https://github.com/tcltk/tcl/tree/core-8-6-15
- Tk: https://github.com/tcltk/tk/tree/core-8-6-15
- Pillow: https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow/tree/10.4.0
- pystray: https://github.com/moses-palmer/pystray/tree/v0.19.5
- six: https://github.com/benjaminp/six/tree/1.17.0
- PyInstaller: https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller/tree/v6.22.3

The standalone EXE is also available separately; download the ZIP to obtain the executable, documentation, and license notices together.
